Privacy Policy - Ozer Assistant

Last updated: September 26, 2026

Ozer Assistant ("the Service") is a personal assistant bot operated by Ozer Labs ("we") for its owner. It communicates over WhatsApp and uses Google APIs to act on the owner's behalf.

Data we access

  • Google Calendar: reading and creating events, to manage the owner's schedule.
  • Gmail (read-only): searching and reading the owner's messages to answer questions and surface follow-ups.
  • Google Drive: reading files the owner asks about, and storing assistant-generated files in a dedicated folder.
  • WhatsApp messages exchanged with the bot.

How we use data

Data is used solely to provide personal assistant services to the account owner: answering questions, managing tasks and reminders, and preparing summaries. Google user data is never used to train models, never sold, and never shared with third parties. The Service's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Storage and retention

Service data is stored on servers hosted by Render (render.com). Data is retained while the Service is active and deleted when the Service is discontinued.

Security

Access tokens and keys are stored in a dedicated secrets vault and in the hosting platform's encrypted environment configuration.
